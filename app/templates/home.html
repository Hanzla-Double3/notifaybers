What it does

Push, no Firebase

Standard Web Push straight to the browser. Works on Android and on iPhones (iOS 16.4+) once added to the home screen.

One-line webhook

Create an API key and call it from a cron job, a deploy script or CI. Your phone buzzes.

Reminders

Once, daily, weekly, monthly or a cron expression, in your own timezone. Describe one in plain words and the optional AI assistant sets it up.

Watchdogs

Give a script a ping URL. If it stops checking in, you get an alert, and another when it recovers.

Utility bills

Enter your consumer number. Get the bill explained in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu, a push when a new one arrives and a reminder before it is due. GEPCO today; every other company is a small plugin anyone can add.

Yours to host

Runs on a VPS or an old phone. Your data stays in one SQLite file you own. Open source under the WTFPL.
